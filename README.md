# 🛒 Retail Store SQL Project

A beginner SQL project that builds and queries a retail store database. This was my first complete SQL project, covering database creation, data insertion, joins, aggregations, subqueries, and filtering.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project simulates a small retail business with customers, products, stores, and orders. I created the full database from scratch and wrote 10 SQL queries to answer real business questions.

---

## 🗄️ Database Structure

The database is called `retail_store` and contains 4 tables:

| Table | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `customers` | Customer name, email, and city |
| `stores` | Store name and location |
| `products` | Product name, category, and price |
| `orders` | Links customers, stores, and products with quantity and date |

---

## 📊 Sample Data

- **6 customers** across Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, and Ibadan
- **4 stores** in major Nigerian cities
- **6 products** across Electronics, Accessories, Appliances, Furniture, and Stationery
- **10 orders** placed between June–July 2023

---

## 🔍 Queries Covered

| # | Question Answered | Concepts Used |
|---|-------------------|---------------|
| 1 | List all customers with their city and email | `SELECT *` |
| 2 | All orders with customer name, product name, and store name | `INNER JOIN` (3 tables) |
| 3 | All customers including those with no orders | `LEFT JOIN` |
| 4 | All products including those never ordered | `RIGHT JOIN` |
| 5 | Total revenue generated by each store | `SUM`, `GROUP BY` |
| 6 | Total products sold by category | `SUM`, `GROUP BY`, `ORDER BY` |
| 7 | Customers who placed more than 2 orders | `HAVING`, `COUNT` |
| 8 | Top 3 customers who spent the most | `ORDER BY`, `LIMIT` |
| 9 | Most expensive product that was actually purchased | Subquery with `MAX` |
| 10 | Customers who bought a Laptop | Nested subqueries |
